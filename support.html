Swooosh! · Funday Morning Games

How to play

Tap a ribbon whose path to the edge is clear in the direction of its arrow: it swooshes out. The ribbons above it fall down. If a ribbon is blocked it bumps and you lose a heart. Some moves would lock the pile; the game rewinds them and you lose a heart. Clear the frame to reveal the picture and add it to your album.

Special ribbons

Golden ribbons give coins. Icy ribbons need two taps. Stones never move. Balloon ribbons float up. Chained ribbons fall together. Bomb ribbons blast nearby stones. In some levels you can rotate the frame: gravity then pulls in a new direction.

Weekly fun

Every week has an event with its own limited picture album, a Journey Pass and a leaderboard (rivals are simulated). Hang your pictures and decorate rooms in My Gallery. Turn on Relaxed mode or color-blind patterns in Settings.

Moving to another device

Settings → Save code → Copy. On the new device: Settings → Save code → paste → Load.
