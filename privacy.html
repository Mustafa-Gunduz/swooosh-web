Privacy Policy

Swooosh! · Funday Morning Games · Last updated: 23 October 2026

Swooosh! is designed to be played without an account. We do not collect, sell or share personal data.

What is stored

Your game progress (level, coins, album, settings) is stored only on your device (browser storage on the web version). You can copy a save code from Settings to move your progress to another device; the code never leaves your device unless you share it.

Advertising and purchases

The current version contains no advertising SDKs, no analytics SDKs and no in-app purchases. If this changes in a future version, this policy will be updated before the change and the game will ask for any consent required by law.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
